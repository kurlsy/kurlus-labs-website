# Kurlus Labs

Kurlus Labs is a founder-led, early-stage software initiative focused on making complex research easier to test, understand, and explain.

**Website:** [kurlus.me](https://kurlus.me) (custom-domain verification pending) · [Live hosted site](https://kurlus-labs.onrender.com)

**Contact:** [kurlus@kurlus.me](mailto:kurlus@kurlus.me)

## First product: StockingAI

StockingAI is a financial market-research and strategy-analysis platform in development. The goal is an evidence-driven workspace that makes methodology, limitations, and risk assumptions visible. The public website currently includes a working, browser-only long-position risk calculator.

We distinguish live product functionality from concepts and development priorities. We do not claim customers, funding, trading performance, or production AI integrations.

## Website

The website is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and deploys automatically to Render Static Sites from `main`.

- `index.html`: company and product overview plus calculator
- `privacy.html`: privacy notice
- `favicon.svg`: site icon
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`: crawlability

## Development status

The company and product are early-stage. The calculator is educational, not investment advice. Hypothetical calculator output is not live market data or audited investment performance.
