Meet StockingAI.

StockingAI is our developing market research and strategy-analysis platform. We're working toward a place where people can examine ideas, study realistic constraints, and better understand what an AI-assisted analysis can—and cannot—tell them.

  • Transparent research and repeatable evaluation workflows
  • Clear distinction between live evidence and illustrations
  • Tools for trade-sizing and scenario exploration
  • AI assistance with clear limits and human review

Our philosophy

Build with ambition.
Validate with rigor.

Good AI software should help people question their assumptions, not simply give them answers that sound confident.

01 / TRANSPARENCY

Show the evidence

Separate actual information from generated interpretation, and make assumptions easier to inspect.

02 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Test the idea

Care about realistic costs, meaningful comparisons, and cases where an apparent edge might fail.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Keep people in control

Surface uncertainty and constraints rather than promising outcomes or automatic success.
